Services

Construction Management
That Supports Better Decisions.

Veridian provides practical owner-side and specialized construction advisory services throughout planning, design, procurement, construction, recovery, claims, and closeout.

A Flexible Delivery Model

Support where the project needs it most.

Services can be structured around a specific phase, a defined need, or ongoing owner-side support. The objective is simple: better information, clearer accountability, and timely action.

Owner's Representation

Senior-level advocacy, decision support, stakeholder coordination, accountability, and owner-side oversight throughout project delivery.

Construction Management

Field observation, progress monitoring, quality assurance, coordination, administration, issue tracking, and closeout support.

Project Recovery & Rescue

Assessment and recovery planning for projects experiencing contractor performance issues, schedule problems, cost pressure, quality concerns, or delivery risk.

Claims & Dispute Support

Contractor claim analysis, contract and record review, entitlement and cost analysis, schedule considerations, and technical support for disputed construction matters.

Surety Advisory

Support for surety coordination and negotiations, project performance issues, recovery strategies, and termination-for-default matters.

Cost Estimating & GMP Review

Independent estimate review, budget validation, GMP analysis, change evaluation, cost monitoring, and cost-control support.

Scheduling & Project Controls

Schedule development and analysis, progress monitoring, reporting, issue tracking, change management, risk mitigation, and project controls.

Federal & Complex Construction Advisory

Technical construction analysis and project support for complex public-sector matters, including claim analysis and support to legal teams in federal court proceedings.

Design & Preconstruction Support

Constructability input, document review, scope development, procurement support, coordination, and readiness for construction.

Quality, Risk & Change Management

Objective identification and documentation of deficiencies, risks, changes, decisions, and corrective actions.

Project Documentation & Closeout

Records, punch lists, warranties, O&M information, record documents, completion tracking, and closeout coordination.

Technical Consulting

Practical technical review and support for construction issues, specifications, field conditions, and project coordination.
