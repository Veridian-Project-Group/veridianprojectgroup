About Veridian

Owner-Side Experience
That Adds Value.

Veridian Project Group is a Colorado-based construction management and owner's representative firm focused on practical project leadership, disciplined controls, and clear communication.

Firm Overview

Senior leadership. Direct accountability.

Veridian provides owner-side project management, construction management representation, cost and schedule management, construction oversight, quality assurance, project controls, and technical consulting for capital improvement projects.

Veridian combines experienced construction management leadership with practical owner-side perspective, technical knowledge, and disciplined project controls. Our approach is designed to provide clients with direct, responsive support throughout planning, design, construction, and closeout.

How We Work

Objective Information. Practical Recommendations.

Veridian helps clients understand what is happening on a project, why it matters, and what decisions or actions may be needed.

Owner Focus

We represent the owner's interests while maintaining professional coordination with contractors, designers, testing firms, regulators, and other stakeholders.

Project Controls

We establish visibility into cost, schedule, changes, risks, issues, documentation, and progress so project leadership can make informed decisions.

Complex Environments

Experience includes public infrastructure, institutional facilities, utilities, water and wastewater systems, historic resources, phased construction, and active operational environments.

Scalable Support

Veridian maintains senior involvement and can add specialized resources when project needs are identified and authorized.
