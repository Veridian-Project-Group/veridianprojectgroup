About Veridian

Owner-Focused.
Practical. Accountable.

Veridian Project Group is a Colorado-based professional services firm focused on construction management, owner's representation, cost and schedule management, project controls, and technical consulting.

Our Role

Veridian serves as an extension of the owner's team, providing objective oversight and practical support throughout planning, design, procurement, construction, and closeout.

Our Focus

We focus on the fundamentals that drive successful project delivery: scope, cost, schedule, quality, risk, coordination, documentation, and clear decisions.

Our Approach

We observe conditions, document the facts, communicate clearly, and support resolution. Our goal is to make project information useful, timely, and actionable.

Our Commitment

Veridian is committed to professional communication, responsive service, objective recommendations, and accountability to the owner's goals.

Built Around the Owner

Clear Information. Better Decisions.

Our services are designed to help owners maintain visibility and control from early planning through successful project closeout.
